What an identity holds, and how to move it. Credits pay for names, documents and contracts — an identity that runs out simply stops.

Credits

Move credits

Top up this identity from a platform address

The address keeps a little back for the transition fee. Leave the amount empty to move everything it holds.

Pay out to a platform address

Signed with the identity's TRANSFER key — the only one allowed to move credits out. There is no identity-to-identity transfer: the other side tops itself up from the address you send to.

Move credits between two platform addresses

Moves the whole balance except a small margin, which stays behind to pay the fee. The destination has to be a different address.

Withdraw back to layer 1
This leaves Platform. Credits become DASH again at the address you name, minimum 0.004 DASH. Withdrawals are not instant — masternodes sign them in batches.
